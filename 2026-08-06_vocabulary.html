Medical Definition
Relating to the myocardium — the muscular wall of the heart; used to describe conditions, damage, or function affecting the heart muscle itself.

心肌的
Common Collocations

Related Words

angioplasty
/ˈæŋ.dʒi.əʊˌplæs.ti/ noun 🔊 Cambridge 📖 M-W Medical
心肺 Cardiopulmonary Procedures & Interventions
Medical Definition
A minimally invasive procedure used to widen narrowed or blocked arteries, typically by threading a balloon catheter into the vessel and inflating it; often performed alongside stent insertion to maintain arterial patency.
繁體中文
血管成形術；氣球擴張術
Common Collocations
coronary angioplasty balloon angioplasty percutaneous angioplasty angioplasty and stenting post-angioplasty care
OET Clinical Example
"Mrs. Patel was referred for cardiac rehabilitation following percutaneous coronary angioplasty and stenting to the left anterior descending artery, performed six weeks prior to this referral."
Related Words
percutaneous coronary intervention / PCI (broader term) stenting (often combined) revascularisation (outcome goal) endarterectomy (surgical alternative) restenosis (recurrence of narrowing)
Word 4 of 5
electrocardiogram
/ɪˌlɛk.trəʊˈkɑː.di.əʊˌɡræm/ noun 🔊 Cambridge 📖 M-W Medical
心肺 Cardiopulmonary Procedures & Interventions
Medical Definition
A graphic record of the electrical activity of the heart, obtained by placing electrodes on the skin; used to diagnose arrhythmias, ischaemia, infarction, and other cardiac conditions. Abbreviated as ECG (British) or EKG (American).
繁體中文
心電圖（ECG）
Common Collocations
12-lead electrocardiogram resting ECG serial electrocardiograms ECG changes abnormal electrocardiogram
OET Clinical Example
"A 12-lead electrocardiogram performed on admission revealed ST-segment elevation in leads II, III, and aVF, consistent with an acute inferior myocardial infarction; the patient was transferred urgently to the catheterisation laboratory."
Related Words
ECG (British abbreviation) EKG (American abbreviation) electrocardiography (the procedure) Holter monitor (24-hour ambulatory ECG) echocardiogram (cardiac ultrasound — different)

pulmonary
/ˈpʌl.mə.nər.i/ adjective 🔊 Cambridge 📖 M-W Medical
心肺 Cardiopulmonary Procedures & Interventions
Medical Definition
Relating to the lungs or to the pulmonary circulation — the circuit by which deoxygenated blood travels from the right heart to the lungs and returns oxygenated to the left heart.
繁體中文
肺（部）的；肺循環的
Common Collocations
pulmonary embolism pulmonary oedema pulmonary rehabilitation pulmonary hypertension pulmonary function tests
OET Clinical Example
"Ms. Nguyen was referred for pulmonary rehabilitation following a recent acute exacerbation of COPD requiring hospitalisation; her baseline six-minute walk test result was 185 metres, indicating severely reduced exercise tolerance."
Related Words
pulmonary embolism / PE (blood clot in lung) pulmonary hypertension (high lung pressure) pulmonary function tests / PFTs cardiac (contrasted — systemic vs pulmonary circulation) bronchopulmonary (bronchi + lungs)
✍️ OET Writing Task — Clinical Scenario (Day 90)

Read the scenario below. Notice how today's vocabulary is used in a realistic OET-style referral context. Highlighted words are from today's list.

Mr. James McCarthy, a 72-year-old retired schoolteacher, was admitted to the coronary care unit following an acute myocardial infarction affecting the inferior wall. An urgent 12-lead electrocardiogram on admission confirmed ST-segment elevation in leads II, III, and aVF, and he subsequently underwent emergency percutaneous coronary angioplasty and stenting to the right coronary artery.

His past medical history is significant for permanent atrial fibrillation and mild pulmonary hypertension, both of which were medically managed prior to this admission. He is currently anticoagulated with apixaban and requires careful heart rate monitoring during all rehabilitation activities.

Mr. McCarthy was referred to the physiotherapy department on Day 4 post-procedure for commencement of phase I cardiac rehabilitation. He was haemodynamically stable, ambulant with supervision, and motivated to participate. Goals of physiotherapy include progressive mobilisation, patient education regarding activity restrictions and symptom monitoring, and coordination of referral to a community-based pulmonary and cardiac rehabilitation programme on discharge.

📌 OET Writing Tip: When documenting a patient with multiple cardiac diagnoses, clearly state each condition and its current management (e.g. "permanent atrial fibrillation managed with anticoagulation"). Specify the exact procedure performed (e.g. "percutaneous coronary angioplasty and stenting") and reference key investigations (e.g. "12-lead electrocardiogram confirmed...") to demonstrate clinical precision in referral letters.
