Word 1 of 5
kinaesthesia
/ˌkɪn.ɪsˈθiː.zi.ə/ noun 🔊 Cambridge 📖 M-W Medical
神經 Neurological 骨科 Musculoskeletal
Medical Definition
The sense that detects body movement, limb position, and the direction and speed of joint motion through receptors in muscles, tendons, and joint capsules; distinct from but related to proprioception.
繁體中文
動覺；肌肉運動感
Common Collocations
impaired kinaesthesia kinaesthesia assessment kinaesthesia retraining kinaesthetic awareness kinaesthetic feedback
OET Clinical Example
"Assessment revealed significantly impaired kinaesthesia in the left knee joint, with the patient unable to accurately reproduce a target angle of 30° of flexion without visual feedback, indicating reduced joint position sense."
Related Words
proprioception (closely related) kinesthesia (American spelling) kinaesthetic (adj) somatosensory (broader category) mechanoreceptor (sensory receptor)

intra-articular
/ˌɪn.trə ɑːˈtɪk.jʊ.lə/ adjective 🔊 Cambridge 📖 M-W Medical
骨科 Musculoskeletal Procedures & Interventions
Medical Definition
Situated within, occurring within, or administered directly into the cavity of a joint; used to describe injections, fractures, pathology, or fluid collections located inside a joint space.
繁體中文
關節內的
Common Collocations
intra-articular injection intra-articular fracture intra-articular pathology intra-articular corticosteroid intra-articular fluid
OET Clinical Example
"The patient received an intra-articular corticosteroid injection to the right hip three weeks prior to this referral, achieving partial symptomatic relief; however, pain during weight-bearing activities has since returned."
Related Words
extra-articular (antonym — outside joint) periarticular (around the joint) articular (of a joint) arthrocentesis (joint aspiration) synovial fluid
Word 3 of 5
retinaculum
/ˌret.ɪˈnæk.jʊ.ləm/ noun 🔊 Cambridge 📖 M-W Medical
骨科 Musculoskeletal
Medical Definition
A fibrous band or sheet of connective tissue that holds tendons, ligaments, or other structures in position; commonly refers to the flexor or extensor retinaculum at the wrist or ankle, or the patellar retinaculum at the knee.
繁體中文
支持帶；腱鞘帶
Common Collocations
flexor retinaculum extensor retinaculum patellar retinaculum retinacular release retinacular tightness
OET Clinical Example
"Ultrasound imaging demonstrated thickening of the flexor retinaculum at the carpal tunnel, consistent with the patient's complaints of nocturnal hand numbness and tingling in a median nerve distribution."
Related Words
retinacula (plural) retinacular (adj) carpal tunnel (flexor retinaculum roof) patellar tracking (patellar retinaculum) fascia (broader connective tissue)

hyperkinesia
/ˌhaɪ.pə.kɪˈniː.zi.ə/ noun 🔊 Cambridge 📖 M-W Medical
神經 Neurological Signs & Symptoms
Medical Definition
Excessive or abnormal involuntary muscle activity; a pathological increase in motor movement that may manifest as choreiform, athetoid, or ballistic movements, often arising from basal ganglia dysfunction or medication side effects.
繁體中文
運動過多症；動作亢進
Common Collocations
drug-induced hyperkinesia tardive hyperkinesia paroxysmal hyperkinesia orolingual hyperkinesia hyperkinesia of the limbs
OET Clinical Example
"The referring neurologist documented drug-induced hyperkinesia characterised by irregular, involuntary movements of the upper limbs and facial grimacing, prompting referral for physiotherapy assessment of functional impact and safety during daily activities."
Related Words
hypokinesia (antonym — reduced movement) dyskinesia (disordered movement) akinesia (absence of movement) bradykinesia (slowness of movement) hyperkinetic (adj)
Word 5 of 5
somatosensory
/ˌsəʊ.mæ.təʊˈsen.sər.i/ adjective 🔊 Cambridge 📖 M-W Medical
神經 Neurological
Medical Definition
Relating to the perception of sensory stimuli arising from the body's surface and internal structures, including touch, pressure, pain, temperature, vibration, and proprioception; processed primarily by the somatosensory cortex of the parietal lobe.
繁體中文
軀體感覺的；體感的
Common Collocations
somatosensory cortex somatosensory deficit somatosensory testing somatosensory impairment somatosensory feedback
OET Clinical Example
"Standardised neurological assessment identified a somatosensory deficit affecting the right lower limb below the knee, with reduced perception of light touch, vibration, and two-point discrimination consistent with peripheral neuropathy."
Related Words
sensorimotor (adj — combined) kinaesthesia (movement sense) nociception (pain sensing) cortical mapping dermatomal distribution
✍️ OET Writing Task — Clinical Scenario (Day 84)

Read the scenario below. Notice how today's vocabulary is used in a realistic OET-style referral context. Highlighted words are from today's list.

Mr. Tama Wirihana, a 54-year-old male with a six-month history of right knee osteoarthritis, was referred to the physiotherapy department following an intra-articular corticosteroid injection administered four weeks ago. While the injection provided initial pain relief, his functional limitations have since returned and he reports ongoing difficulty negotiating stairs and walking on uneven terrain.

On examination, joint position sense testing revealed markedly impaired kinaesthesia in the right knee, with the patient demonstrating an average error of 8° during passive angle reproduction tasks at 30° of flexion. MRI imaging from the referring orthopaedic surgeon indicated lateral retinaculum tightness contributing to lateral patellar maltracking and associated patellofemoral pain.

A comprehensive somatosensory assessment was completed, confirming intact superficial sensation but deficits in vibration perception and joint movement detection bilaterally, warranting further neurological review.

Physiotherapy will focus on kinaesthesia retraining, quadriceps and hip abductor strengthening, patellar taping, and patient education regarding activity modification and joint protection strategies.

📌 OET Writing Tip: When documenting sensory deficits in referral letters, specify the modality affected (e.g. light touch, vibration, proprioception), the distribution (e.g. right lower limb below the knee), and the functional impact (e.g. impaired balance on uneven terrain). Precise sensory language demonstrates high clinical literacy in OET Writing tasks.
